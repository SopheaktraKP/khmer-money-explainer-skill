# Visual style

The look: a clean, light stage with warm accents. Inked stick characters, big readable Khmer, and money made physical as jars, bags, tags and bills. 1920x1080, 30 fps.

## Language of the frame

- **One focal idea at a time.** A viewer watching on a phone should get the point of a frame in a second.
- **Recurring stage.** Keep the same characters and setting in the same places through a chapter, and add overlays on top. The continuity makes long videos feel calm. Change the setting when the chapter changes.
- **Something changes every 3-6 seconds:** a tag pops, a figure reacts, a bar grows. Never a static slide. The stage's slow camera creep and the figures' idle motion handle the in-between.
- **Reactions sell the story.** Change faces and poses on the key word. A character's face should tell you whether the news is good or bad with the sound off.
- **Colour means something.** Teal/green for gain and saving, coral/pink for cost and loss, sun yellow for attention and neutral prices. Each main character keeps one body colour for the whole video.
- **Mood through the stage.** `warm` for hope or stories, `cool` for the calm option, and `stress` grey for bad news. Use `tint` so the stage turns grey right on the bad word.
- **Numbers on cards.** Comparisons go on a white `Card` with bars that grow in time with the narration. Totals land with an SFX.
- **Show your workings.** Use `Note` for assumptions and sources, and leave it up long enough to read.

## Common visual metaphors

| Idea | Visual |
|---|---|
| Savings | `Jar` filling up |
| Debt, burden | `Rock` on the head or shoulders |
| Monthly payments, time | `MonthGrid`, `Calendar` |
| Money leaving | `FlyingMoney` arcing away |
| A big sum | `MoneyBag` with its amount |
| Bad event | `StormCloud` and `stress` tint |
| Safety net | `Umbrella` over a jar |
| Which way? | Split stage with `VsBadge` |

## Thumbnail

Bold Khmer words naming the two sides, a split background, `VsBadge`, one shocked or thrilled character, and 2-3 money tags. Show a tension that the video answers. Readable at phone size: 7 words at most. Keep claims true to the video.

## Frame checklist

Run this on stills before voicing and on QA sheets before delivery:

- [ ] Is any Khmer text cut off, overlapped, or broken by its background?
- [ ] Do subscripts and vowels render properly (no dotted circles)?
- [ ] Does any tag, title or card cover a face?
- [ ] Is anything important within 60 px of the edge?
- [ ] Does the chapter bar collide with a title?
- [ ] Can you tell who's winning with the sound off?
- [ ] Do the figures match `numbers.json`?
- [ ] Are characters the same as in the previous scene?
- [ ] Is any object arriving before its word, or more than half a second after it?
- [ ] Is any card or stage empty for more than a second? Put a calendar, headline or the first item in it while the voice gets to the number.
